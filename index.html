About My Passion

Welcome to MakerMind Nexus! I am passionate about 3D printing and believe that everyone should have the opportunity to explore this exciting hobby. That's why I am dedicated to helping those less fortunate by providing them with the machines and materials they need to get started.

I take old 3D printers and rehab them to give them a new life. My goal is to make 3D printing accessible to everyone, regardless of their financial situation. I document my journey of rehabbing these machines on my YouTube channel, so you can follow along and learn with me.

2. Shipping and Associated Costs

The Company is responsible for all costs and logistics associated with shipping the product to MakerMind Nexus, including but not limited to packaging, handling, and delivery fees.

3. Review Timeline

While we strive to publish reviews in a timely manner, no specific publication date is guaranteed. The review schedule is at the discretion of MakerMind Nexus and depends on current content priorities.

4. Editorial Independence and Content

MakerMind Nexus maintains full editorial control over the content of the review. All reviews will be honest, objective, and based on our genuine experience with the product. The Company will not have the right to approve or edit the review before it is published. Any issues or defects discovered during testing will be communicated to the Company as a professional courtesy.

5. Content Sharing and Distribution

The review, including unboxing and usage, will be shared on the MakerMind Nexus YouTube channel and may be promoted across other social media platforms, such as those listed on our Linktree.

6. FTC Disclosure

In compliance with FTC guidelines, all review content will include a clear disclosure stating that the product was provided by the Company for review purposes.

7. Liability

MakerMind Nexus is not liable for any damage that may occur to the product during the testing and review process.

8. No Guaranteed Positive Outcome

We do not guarantee a positive review. The outcome and tone of the review will be determined solely by our experience with the product's performance, quality, and features.

By sending your product, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.

What We Offer

Are you looking to get your product in front of a passionate, hands-on community of makers, creators, and hobbyists? MakerMind Nexus is here to help you showcase your products across a range of content platforms, including YouTube, live streams, social media, and more!

  • In-Depth Product Reviews: Your product will be featured in a detailed, honest review on our YouTube channel, MakerMind Nexus, where we dive deep into the functionality, quality, and real-world use of the item.
  • Unboxing on Live Streams: We love sharing first impressions with our audience. Your product will be unboxed live on stream, giving viewers a raw and authentic look at what they can expect when they purchase it.
  • Broad Social Media Reach: In addition to YouTube and live streams, we’ll share content across all our social platforms, including Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, and TikTok. Our followers trust our recommendations, and your product will be seen by an engaged audience eager to learn about new tools and technologies.

Why Collaborate with Us?

MakerMind Nexus is more than just a tech channel—our mission is to help others, particularly those less fortunate, gain access to the world of making and 3D printing. By partnering with us, you’re not only showcasing your product but aligning with a community-driven initiative that appeals to makers from all walks of life. We’ll help you tap into an audience that values innovation, creativity, and practicality, potentially reaching people you might not have thought to target.

How It Works

  • Products are reviewed and tested thoroughly. We’ll provide constructive feedback and honest insights, ensuring that our audience gets a genuine perspective.
  • Your products become the property of MakerMind Nexus unless otherwise agreed upon, allowing us to integrate them into our ongoing projects and content.
  • We handle all the promotional content creation, with companies covering shipping costs.

We’re confident that our approach to highlighting products will resonate with our community and help your brand reach a wider, more diverse audience.
