About MakerMind Nexus

MakerMind Nexus is the home of creativity, restoration, and accessible innovation. Founded by Dan Borio, the channel and community are dedicated to breathing new life into old 3D printers, empowering makers of all skill levels, and helping bring technology within reach for those who need it most.

What started as a passion for tinkering has grown into a mission: rehab, rebuild, and re-imagine. Whether it’s salvaging neglected machines, sharing repair know-how, or donating equipment and materials to people who can’t afford to get started, MakerMind Nexus exists to make the world of 3D printing more open and inclusive.

On the MakerMind Nexus YouTube channel, Dan documents each restoration journey—from diagnosis to rebuild to final print—so you can learn the process, avoid the pitfalls, and gain confidence in your own maker projects. Every machine has a story, and every repair is a chance to teach, inspire, and help grow the maker community.

Beyond repairs, MakerMind Nexus fosters a space where curiosity thrives. Here you’ll find discussions on technology, creative problem-solving, and the maker mindset that encourages everyone to keep exploring, keep learning, and keep building.

Whether you’re here to learn, to be inspired, or to breathe new life into a forgotten printer, welcome to the Nexus. Let’s make something awesome together.

1. Ownership of Products

All products sent for review will become the property of Dan Borio (MakerMind Nexus) unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing before shipment.

2. Shipping Costs

The Company is responsible for covering all shipping costs, including but not limited to packaging, handling, and delivery fees, to ensure the product reaches MakerMind Nexus.

3. Unboxing and Reviews

All products will be unboxed live on the MakerMind Nexus YouTube channel (https://www.youtube.com/@makermindnexus) during scheduled streams. Reviews will be honest and transparent. Any issues or defects discovered during the review process will be promptly communicated to the Company for their awareness.

4. Content Sharing

In addition to the YouTube review, the product's unboxing, usage, and feedback will be shared across all MakerMind Nexus social media platforms. Links to these platforms can be found at https://linktr.ee/makermindnexus.

5. No Guaranteed Outcome

While all reviews aim to be thorough and objective, no guarantees are made regarding the outcome or tone of the review. All opinions shared will reflect the honest experience of MakerMind Nexus with the product.
